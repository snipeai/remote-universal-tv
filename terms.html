Remote Universal for All TV Terms of Service

Last updated 23 November 2026. Developer: Hanek Dev.

The service

Remote Universal for All TV is a remote-control app for compatible televisions on your local network. Compatibility depends on your TV's model, software and settings and on manufacturer protocols that can change. Not every feature works on every TV.

Free version

TV discovery, pairing, power where supported, volume, channels, navigation, home/back, basic playback, basic text entry where supported, TV management, connection troubleshooting and basic casting are free and will remain free. The free version shows ads. Ads are never placed over the remote controls and never interrupt pairing or purchases.

Premium

Premium removes all ads and unlocks additional themes and customisation, advanced gestures, advanced and custom automation, multi-TV and voice features, premium widgets and other convenience features listed in the app.

Monthly Premium (subscription)

  • Charged every month at the price shown in the app at the time of purchase (US list price $2.99 per month; prices vary by country and include taxes where required).
  • Renews automatically until cancelled. Cancel at any time in Google Play or App Store subscription settings, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. After cancelling you keep Premium until the end of the paid period.
  • No free trial is currently offered.

Lifetime Premium (one-time purchase)

A single payment (US list price $9.99) that unlocks the same Premium features on the store account used to buy it, with no subscription and no recurring charges, for as long as the app and the purchased features are offered.

Optional rewarded ads

In the free version you may choose to watch a short ad to use the app without ads for 2 hours. This is optional and grants no other benefit.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple under their policies. A refunded purchase no longer unlocks Premium.

Acceptable use

Use the app only with devices you own or are allowed to control.

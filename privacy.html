Remote Universal for All TV Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026. Developer: Hanek Dev.

Summary

Remote Universal for All TV controls TVs on your own home network. It has no account system and does not send your TV data to our servers; we do not operate servers that receive your data. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Premium (monthly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase) removes all ads.

Data that stays on your phone

  • TVs you add: name, model, network address, room and pairing credentials. Pairing credentials are stored encrypted with the operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Your settings, layouts, favourite apps and automation scenes.
  • Usage counters kept only on your phone (for example how many sessions, how many ads were shown, whether the Premium screen was opened). They are never uploaded automatically and contain no identifiers. They are included in a diagnostic report only if you choose to share one.
  • A short diagnostic log in memory. It is never sent automatically.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the app set ID and the Android advertising ID. The advertising ID lets Google show ads that may be personalised (based on your interests and activity in other apps) and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) ads are personalised only if you agree in Google's consent message; otherwise non-personalised ads are shown. On iOS the app does not request tracking. The app is intended for adults (18+). Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where required by US state privacy laws, the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown when it applies to you).
  • No ads for Premium: if you have an active Premium subscription or the lifetime purchase, the app does not start the ad SDK, request ads or show the consent message. (The SDK library is part of the app for everyone.)
  • Optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch a short video ad to use the app without ads for 1 hour. This is always optional.
  • Ad content is limited to ads rated for general audiences (G), and every ad request follows Google Play's Families rules for younger users, because the app may be used by teenagers.

Local network

The app talks directly to TVs on your Wi-Fi to discover and control them. These connections stay inside your home network.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you use the voice button. Speech is converted to text by your phone's own speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS), which may process audio under that provider's privacy policy. The app does not record or store audio.

Media casting

When you cast photos, videos or music, the app runs a temporary server on your phone that only the selected TV can reach, using a one-time secret link. It stops when casting ends. Files are not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive no payment card details. The app stores your purchase status, encrypted, on your phone so Premium keeps working briefly when you are offline.

Diagnostic reports and feedback

Only if you choose to share a report. Reports remove passwords, pairing keys, tokens and network addresses before you see them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Settings > Ad privacy choices: change or withdraw ad consent (where it applies).
  • Android settings > Privacy > Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Settings > Privacy > Forget all removes every saved TV and credential.
  • Premium removes all ads. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

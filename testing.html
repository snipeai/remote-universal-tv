Help test Remote Universal for All TV

Remote Universal for All TV is a free universal TV remote for Android by Hanek Dev, an independent developer. Before it can be published on Google Play, Google requires a closed beta: at least 12 real testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row. Your help gets the app launched, and your TV reports decide which TVs we support best.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer and the Google Play Store.
  • About 10 minutes on day 1, then a quick open of the app every few days.
  • Ideally, a smart TV on the same Wi-Fi: Google TV / Android TV (TCL, Sony, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi…), Samsung, LG, Vizio or Sony BRAVIA. No TV? You can still test the app's screens, languages and settings.

How to join (3 steps)

  1. Join the tester group with the same Google account you use in the Play Store on your phone:
    Join the tester group
  2. Opt in to the beta (same Google account):
    Become a tester
    The page may take up to a few hours to work after you join the group.
  3. Install the app from the Play Store link on that page, and keep it installed and stay opted in for 14 days.

Test checklist

Day 1 (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open the app and go through the welcome screens.
  2. Let it search your Wi-Fi for TVs. Does it find your TV? If not, try Devices › + to add it by IP address.
  3. Pair with your TV (Google TV / Android TV shows a code on the TV screen; Samsung and LG ask you to accept on the TV).
  4. Try the D-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and channel keys.
  5. Switch to Touchpad and Numbers at the top of the remote.
  6. Tap an app in the app row (YouTube, Netflix…) and see if it opens on the TV.
  7. Try the keyboard button in a TV search box, and the microphone button.
  8. Open Settings and change the theme or language.

Days 2–14

  • Use it as your normal remote a few times. Try casting a photo, a scene in Automation, or a home-screen widget.
  • Update the app when the Play Store offers a new beta version: we fix reported problems during the test.

How to send feedback

  • TV compatibility report (most useful): your TV brand, model and year, and what worked or didn't. Fill in the TV report (GitHub account needed) or email it.
  • Bug or idea: report on GitHub, or use Google Play's private feedback on the app page.
  • Email: hanekdevolpes@gmail.com – include your phone model and TV model.

Good to know

  • The app is free to test. The beta shows test ads on some screens (never on the remote itself). You never need to buy anything.
  • We only see the Google account email you join with (through the group and Google Play). We never share it, and you can leave the group at any time.
  • The app does not support Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV or VIDAA TVs yet.
  • Privacy: Privacy Policy.
